DIVINE SILICON ENGINEERING

Three Prongs, One Purpose

Purpose-built CUDA kernels for the Pearl proof-of-work, written for sm_86 and nothing else — the matrix stage is being built against a CPU oracle ported from the real Pearl GPU source, so the math is verified before it is fast.

GDDR6X Abyssal Straps

Tuning profiles for the 3090's GDDR6X and core clocks, starting from a fixed bench profile (core lock 1600 MHz, +200 offset, 810 MHz memory floor — plus a 405 MHz memory-lock arm). Every profile is a hypothesis until a pool-side bench proves it; no strap promises numbers here.

Leviathan Thermal Guard

Core-clock locking and thermal guardrails hold the card inside its power envelope — the design target is ≤ 320 W at the wall while chasing ≥ 125 TH/s. Targets, stated as targets: the bench decides what is real.

Zero-Latency Pearl Stratum

A lean stratum client with pool failover, built to switch jobs fast and count stale shares honestly — they are reported separately in the stats, never hidden inside the accepted total.

TRANSPARENCY

        
Dev Fee — 2%, In the Open

        
Poseidon 3090 charges a 2% dev fee the standard way — the same mechanism as SRBMiner, OneZeroMiner, lolMiner and WildRig: every so often the miner switches to the dev pool and mines to the wallet below for 2% of the time, then switches back to your pool. Fee rounds are announced in the log and stats while they run, and a round is skipped rather than ever interrupting your mining. No hidden fee, ever.
