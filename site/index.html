Architecture

Why another Pearl miner?

Pure Pearl

Zero multi-coin bloat or fallback branches. Every instruction, buffer, and scheduling decision is dedicated solely to Pearl (PRL).

Pure 3090

Tuned exclusively for the Nvidia RTX 3090 (Ampere SM count, register file, and GDDR6X memory behavior) instead of lowest-common-denominator kernels.

The GPU never stalls

Pipelined work dispatch and asynchronous host-device coordination keep execution units fed continuously with minimal idle gaps.

Real autotune

Measured per-card tuning that adapts to your exact silicon and thermal envelope rather than shipping static one-size-fits-all presets.

Transparency

Dev fee — 2%, in the open

Poseidon 3090 charges a 2% dev fee the standard way — the same mechanism as SRBMiner, OneZeroMiner, lolMiner and WildRig: every so often the miner switches to the dev pool and mines to the wallet below for 2% of the time, then switches back to your pool. Fee rounds are announced in the log and stats while they run, and a round is skipped rather than ever interrupting your mining. No hidden fee, ever.
